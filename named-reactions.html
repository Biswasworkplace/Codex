High-Yield Named Reactions

Aldol Condensation

Enolate adds to carbonyl to make β-hydroxy carbonyl, then dehydration gives α,β-unsaturated product.

Grignard Reaction

RMgX adds to carbonyl compounds; acidic workup forms alcohols.

Friedel-Crafts Alkylation

Electrophilic aromatic substitution installs alkyl groups on activated aromatic rings.

Diels-Alder

[4+2] cycloaddition between diene and dienophile gives cyclohexene derivatives.

Cannizzaro

Non-enolizable aldehydes disproportionate to alcohol + carboxylate in strong base.

Wittig

Phosphonium ylide converts aldehydes/ketones into alkenes.
