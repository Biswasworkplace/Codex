4-Week Organic Chemistry Plan

Week 1

Structure, bonding, acidity/basicity, resonance, and hybridization drills.

Week 2

Functional groups, stereochemistry, and conformational analysis.

Week 3

Substitution/elimination mechanisms and alkene/alkyne reactions.

Week 4

Carbonyl chemistry, spectroscopy, and mixed mock tests with error log review.

Daily Format (90 minutes)

  • 20 min revision from previous day
  • 45 min targeted questions
  • 15 min mechanism writing practice
  • 10 min formula and exception revision
