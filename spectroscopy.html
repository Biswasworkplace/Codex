Spectroscopy Solver

IR

  • O-H broad: 3200-3600 cm⁻¹
  • C=O strong: 1650-1750 cm⁻¹
  • C-H sp2/sp3: 2850-3100 cm⁻¹

¹H NMR

  • Chemical shift tells proton environment.
  • Splitting approximately follows n+1 rule.
  • Integration gives proton ratio.

¹³C NMR

  • Number of signals equals unique carbons.
  • Carbonyl carbons often downfield (160+ ppm).
  • Proton-decoupled spectra usually singlets.

MS

  • M⁺ suggests molecular mass.
  • Base peak is most stable fragment.
  • M+2 pattern often implies halogens.
