Organic Chemistry Glossary

Electrophile
Electron-pair acceptor; usually electron-deficient.
Nucleophile
Electron-pair donor; often has lone pair or pi electrons.
Resonance
Delocalization model where multiple valid Lewis forms contribute to real structure.
Hyperconjugation
Stabilization via overlap of sigma bonds with adjacent empty or partially filled orbitals.
Regioselectivity
Preference for bond formation at one position over alternatives.
Stereoselectivity
Preference for formation of one stereoisomer over another.
