Student Resources

Lab Safety Essentials

  • Wear PPE: goggles, coat, gloves.
  • Label all containers clearly.
  • Follow waste segregation rules.

Practice Workflow

  • Solve by topic first.
  • Move to mixed papers later.
  • Track mistakes in error notebook.

Memory Tools

  • Flashcards for reagents.
  • Mechanism maps for pathways.
  • Weekly spaced revision cycles.
