Reaction Mechanism Master Page

SN1

Two-step via carbocation; racemization possible. Favored by tertiary substrates and polar protic solvents.

SN2

Single-step backside attack; inversion of configuration. Favored by primary substrates and strong nucleophiles.

E1/E2

E1 parallels SN1. E2 needs anti-periplanar arrangement and strong base; often gives Zaitsev alkene.

Carbonyl Addition

Nucleophile attacks electrophilic carbonyl carbon. Protonation or elimination steps follow depending on conditions.

Arrow-Pushing Rules

  1. Arrows originate from lone pairs, pi bonds, or negative charge.
  2. Never exceed octet for second-row elements.
  3. Track charge after every step.
  4. Check regioselectivity and stereochemistry in final product.
